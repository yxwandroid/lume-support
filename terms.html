Usage Guide

Effective October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

These notes explain using Lume 1.0.0. Downloads and use through the App Store are subject to the Apple Standard End User License Agreement. This page does not replace Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement.

1. This version

All cameras, filters, accessories and creative tools in Lume 1.0.0 are free. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. No account is required. Available cameras, physical lenses and formats depend on your iPhone hardware and current capture configuration.

2. Your media

Photos and videos you capture, import or edit remain yours or the original rights holder’s. Lume does not take ownership of them. Use media you own or have permission to process, and respect the wishes and privacy of people you photograph. Processing is local; you choose what to share and export.

3. Effects and formats

Retro colors, grain and digital accessories are original simulations. RAW, native Live Photos, depth and Log require compatible hardware; ordinary photos or a demonstration scene cannot replace those formats. Digital dimming only darkens the image and does not extend sensor exposure. Use the viewfinder and result previews to adjust the look.

4. Saving and backups

Your library is stored on your device. Edit versions and source retention depend on your save settings. Some effects cannot be reprocessed after an original is removed. Removing the app deletes its local data; export work and backups first. Copies in Photos, file providers or system backups are managed separately.

5. Permissions and privacy

Permissions are requested when needed and can be managed in iOS Settings. See the Privacy Policy for data handling, optional diagnostics and support email.

6. Updates and support

The effective date will change when these notes are updated. For help or privacy questions, contact yxw_flutter@163.com.
