Lume 是一款在 iPhone 上拍摄、编辑和保存照片与复古视频的相机。1.0.0 版本全部功能免费，无广告、订阅或内购。

联系支持

使用问题、意见或隐私请求，请发送邮件至 yxw_flutter@163.com。建议附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、Lume 版本及复现步骤。请只提供解决问题所需的信息；无需发送私人照片、精确位置或账号密码。

邮件由你主动发送。若附带截图、原片或诊断文件，请先检查并移除不希望分享的内容。

怎样切换相机、滤镜和配件？

点取景页右下角的相机图标，打开相机架。横向浏览两行相机，选中后面板会保持打开；可继续选择配件、画幅、相框和色彩，最后点下箭头应用并回到取景。

配件旁的调节入口可改变强度及具体参数。不同相机支持的配件、色彩与格式不同。胶片滤镜与质感可从取景页的更多菜单或照片编辑器进入。

没有相机权限，可以编辑吗？

可以。打开左下角作品库，点右上角添加照片图标，通过系统照片选择器导入素材后进行编辑。只导入你主动选择的照片，不读取整个系统照片库。

拍摄时才需要相机权限；录制有声视频需要麦克风。你可以选择静音录制。在 iOS「设置」中可修改权限及应用语言。

为什么有些格式或相机没有显示？

RAW、实况照片、深度摄影、Log 与物理镜头依赖设备和当前配置。不支持的特殊相机默认隐藏；可用功能以应用检测结果为准。数字配件为成像效果，数字减光不会延长感光元件曝光。

保存、导出与备份

拍摄和导入的素材保存在应用内作品库。你可保存成片到系统相册，或通过系统分享导出成片、原片和备份；首次存入相册时需允许添加照片。拒绝此权限时，作品仍保存在应用内，可通过系统分享导出。

在「设置 → 作品与空间 → 完整备份与恢复」创建备份。卸载应用会删除设备上的应用资料，请先将需要的作品和备份保存到合适位置。已导出的系统相册或其他位置副本独立保留。系统 iCloud 备份或 iCloud 照片是否保存这些资料，由你的 Apple 设置决定。

反馈问题时如何提供诊断？

「设置 → 使用说明 → 诊断与反馈」可以主动生成并完整预览 JSON。它包含应用/系统版本、设备类型、空间与作品数量、处理错误类别数量、相机目录和引擎版本，不包含照片、视频、位置、文件路径、账号、设备名称或具体错误内容。生成和预览不会发送；只有你选用系统分享并确认发送给某个接收方时，文件才会交给该接收方。

Support

Updated October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Lume is an iPhone camera for capturing, editing and saving photos and retro-style video. All features in version 1.0.0 are free, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Contact support

For help, feedback or privacy requests, email yxw_flutter@163.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Lume version and steps to reproduce the issue when useful. Provide only what is needed to solve the problem. Private photos, precise locations and account passwords are not required.

You choose whether to send an email. Before attaching a screenshot, original or diagnostic file, review it and remove anything you do not want to share.

How do I switch cameras, filters and accessories?

Tap the camera icon at the bottom right of the viewfinder to open the camera shelf. Browse the two rows horizontally. The shelf stays open after a selection, so you can keep choosing accessories, framing and color. Tap the down arrow to apply your choices and return to the viewfinder.

Use the accessory adjustment entry to change strength and specific settings. Available accessories, colors and formats vary by camera. Open film filters and textures from the viewfinder more menu or the photo editor.

Can I edit without Camera permission?

Yes. Open the library using the bottom-left thumbnail, then tap the photo-plus button at the top right to import an image with the system Photos picker. Only photos you select are imported; Lume does not read your entire Photos library.

Camera access is needed for capture. Microphone access is needed for video with sound; silent recording is available. Manage permissions and app language in iOS Settings.

Why are some cameras or formats unavailable?

RAW, Live Photos, depth photography, Log and physical lenses depend on your device and the current configuration. Unsupported specialized cameras are hidden by default. The app uses detected capabilities. Digital accessories are image effects; digital dimming does not extend sensor exposure.

Saving, exporting and backing up

Captured and imported media stay in the local app library. Save finished work to Photos, or use system sharing to export finished work, originals and backups. Saving to Photos needs add-only permission. If it is denied, your work remains in the app and can still be exported with system sharing.

Create a backup in Settings → Artwork and Storage → Full Backup and Restore. Removing the app deletes its on-device data, so first export the work and backups you want to keep. Copies already exported to Photos or other destinations remain separate. Your Apple settings determine whether iCloud Backup or iCloud Photos stores these files.

How can I provide diagnostics?

Settings → Help → Diagnostics and Feedback can generate a JSON file with a full preview. It contains app/system versions, device type, storage and artwork counts, counts of processing error categories, and camera catalog/engine versions. It excludes photos, videos, location, file paths, accounts, device names and detailed error messages. Generating or previewing it does not send it. It is provided to a recipient only if you choose system sharing and send it.
